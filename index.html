Unit 1 & 2 Core Grammar

Grammar 1: Can for requests and offers & Place Prepositions

In this unit, we learn how to politely ask for assistance using "Can", locate places around our town, and give clear directions. Let's review the structures below!

🤝 Asking for Help & Requests

Question:

Can you help me? / Sure. How can I help?

Asking for locations:

Where's the [supermarket / museum]?

We use Can at the beginning of the sentence to make polite requests and offers in public places.

📍 Prepositions of Place

  • on Main Street Street location
  • next to the police station Beside
  • across from the bakery Opposite side
  • between the park and school In the middle
  • on the corner of 1st & Main Intersection

Grammar 2: Giving Directions

When someone asks "How can I get to the post office?", we give directions using movement verbs and prepositions:

⬆️

Go straight

Walk forward along the street.

↗️

Turn right

Change direction to your right side.

↖️

Turn left

Change direction to your left side.

Advanced Level Multi-turn Requests & Complex Routing

Example 11 (Advanced): Tourist asking for two locations

Dialogue:
- Can you help me? Sure. How can I help?
- Where's the library?
- It's on First Street, next to the police station. And if you want the museum, go straight and turn right on Second Street!

Example 12 (Advanced): Navigating from school to zoo

Directions:
- Start at the school on White Street. Go straight, turn left on First Street, walk past the hospital, turn right on Green Street, and the zoo is on your right across from the post office.

Example 13 (Advanced): Finding the toy store

Directions:
- Go straight on First Street between the library and the mall. Turn right on White Street. The toy store is between the drugstore and the school.

Example 14 (Advanced): Visiting the movie theater

Directions:
- Walk down Second Street, turn left on White Street. The movie theater is right across from the school and next to the museum.

Example 15 (Advanced): Emergency route to hospital

Directions:
- From the stadium, go straight down First Street. The hospital is immediately on your left, across from the park swimming pool.

Practice Exercises (15 Total with Hidden Step-by-Step Solutions)

Basic 1

Complete the request dialogue:

"Can you help ______? Sure. How can I ______? Where's the supermarket?"

Step-by-step Solution:

1. First blank: me ("Can you help me?")

2. Second blank: help ("How can I help?")

Basic 2

Fill in the preposition for the post office location:

"Where's the post office? It is ______ the police station." (Hint: It is right next to it)

Step-by-step Solution:

1. Look at the map position relative to the police station.

2. Correct answer: next to.

Basic 3

Identify the correct street preposition:

"The museum is ______ Main Street."

Step-by-step Solution:

1. When referring to a specific street name, we use the preposition on.

2. Answer: on Main Street.

Basic 4

Complete the bakery location:

"The toy store is ______ the bakery." (Opposite side of the street)

Step-by-step Solution:

1. The opposite side is indicated by across from.

2. Answer: across from.

Basic 5

Complete the intersection location:

"It's ______ the corner of Main Street and First Street."

Step-by-step Solution:

1. For corners, the correct phrase is on.

2. Answer: on the corner of...

Intermediate 6

Fill in the directions to the stadium:

"How can I get to the stadium? Go ______ on First Street. Turn ______ on Green Street."

Step-by-step Solution:

1. Moving forward along a street: straight.

2. Making a directional change: right.

Intermediate 7

Complete the direction phrase:

"It's on the corner ______ Green Street and Second Street."

Step-by-step Solution:

1. We connect two streets on a corner using and.

2. Answer: and.

Intermediate 8

Complete the direction to the post office:

"Go straight on First Street. Turn ______ on Green Street. It's ______ the zoo."

Step-by-step Solution:

1. First blank: left.

2. Second blank: across from.

Intermediate 9

Fill in the missing words for asking directions:

"______ can I get to the museum?"

Step-by-step Solution:

1. The question word for asking directions is How.

2. Answer: How can I get to the museum?

Intermediate 10

Identify the turn direction:

"Turn ______ at the supermarket." (Pointing towards the left arrow ↖️)

Step-by-step Solution:

1. Symbol ↖️ indicates left.

2. Answer: left.

Advanced 11

Complete the full multi-step dialogue:

"Can you ______ me? Sure. How can I help? Where's the train station? It is ______ White Street, next to the school."

Step-by-step Solution:

1. First blank: help.

2. Second blank: on (street preposition).

Advanced 12

Construct directions combining two actions:

"How can I get to the post office? Go ______ on First Street and turn ______ on Green Street."

Step-by-step Solution:

1. First blank: straight.

2. Second blank: right (or left depending on map route).

Advanced 13

Identify complex positioning:

"The park is ______ the toy store ______ the school." (Two prepositions for sandwich position)

Step-by-step Solution:

1. Structure for being in the middle of two things: between ... and ...

2. Answer: between ... and ...

Advanced 14

Fix the grammar mistake:

"Where are the museum?" -> Correct the sentence.

Step-by-step Solution:

1. Museum is singular, so we use "Where's" (Where is).

2. Correct sentence: Where's the museum?

Advanced 15

Complete the full navigation instruction:

"To reach the zoo, go ______ on First Street, turn right ______ Green Street, and it is ______ the post office."

Step-by-step Solution:

1. First blank: straight.

2. Second blank: on.

3. Third blank: across from (or next to).

Formative Evaluation with Hidden Solutions

Review test-style questions to assess your mastery of Unit 1 & 2 grammar.

Quiz 1

Choose the correct question to ask for a place location:

A) Where you are museum?

B) Where's the museum?

C) How museum is?

Correct Answer: B) Where's the museum?

Explanation: "Where's" is the contraction of "Where is", which is the correct structure for singular places.

Quiz 2

If a building is situated directly between the park and the school, which preposition describes it?

A) across from

B) between

C) on

Correct Answer: B) between

Explanation: "Between" is used when an object is in the middle of two other locations.

Quiz 3

Which direction command tells you to walk forward along a street?

A) Turn left

B) Go straight

C) Turn right

Correct Answer: B) Go straight

Explanation: "Go straight" means to move forward in the same direction.
